The ritual

Five minutes.
Four small steps.

No perfect routine required. Just a pause you can return to, morning or night.

i.

Measure

One heaped teaspoon of loose leaf per cup. Notice the colors and scent.

ii.

Steep

Just-boiled water, covered, for 5–7 minutes. Put your phone down while it brews.

iii.

Breathe

Hands around the cup. Three slow breaths before the first sip.

iv.

Return

Back to your day — or your book — a little more yourself.

Our story

Born from curiosity,
steeped in respect.

Luna Jing began at a kitchen table in Minneapolis, when founder Anna went looking for gentler ways to care for herself — and found herself drawn to herbal traditions and Traditional Chinese Medicine.

“This brand isn't built on knowing everything. It's built on a commitment to keep learning — and to share what I learn honestly.”
精

Why “Jing”? In Traditional Chinese Medicine, jing (精) describes one's essence — the deep reserve of vitality we're encouraged to protect and replenish. Paired with luna, the moon, it's our reminder to move with rhythm and to rest before we run on empty.
