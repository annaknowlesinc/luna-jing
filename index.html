The heart of Luna Jing

Your body was never meant to be a mystery

For thousands of years, women observed nature's rhythms and recognized those same rhythms within themselves. Their menstrual cycles were not inconveniences to suppress — they were seasons to understand, honor, and work alongside.

Modern life often asks women to perform the same every day, regardless of where they are in their cycle.

Luna Jing offers another way.
One season. One phase. One cup at a time.

Wisdom

We honor thousands of years of Traditional Chinese Medicine while embracing thoughtful, evidence-informed learning.

Education

Rhythm

Nature already understands balance. Our bodies do too. Luna Jing is a lifelong invitation to live in rhythm.

A
Founder
Anna
Minneapolis, Minnesota

Luna Jing was born from personal curiosity. After years of navigating health challenges and searching for answers, I began exploring holistic wellness, herbal traditions, and Traditional Chinese Medicine.

"Chinese medicine is rooted in ancient traditions and holistic principles that view the body, mind, heart, and spirit as interconnected."

Luna Jing became the answer. This company is not built on the idea that I know everything — it is built on a commitment to lifelong learning. As Luna Jing grows, I plan to pursue formal education and certification in TCM so this brand continues to evolve with integrity, humility, and deep respect for one of the world's oldest healing systems.

Our vision

A future where women understand their bodies as naturally as they understand the changing seasons

Where periods are discussed without shame. Where hormonal changes are expected instead of feared. Where wellness is rooted in education instead of confusion.
